    Tips for a good identification
    • Fill the frame with one subject. One flower, one leaf or one bird, not the whole garden.
    • Get the key parts in view: the face and breast of a bird, the cap and gills of a mushroom, a whole leaf.
    • Avoid heavy zoom and backlight. Step closer and keep the sun behind you.
    • Not sure? Check the other options. The right answer is often second. Tap it to switch.

    Setup

    Download once, use offline

    Both models run inside your browser. Download them once on Wi-Fi and the app keeps working on a trail with no signal. Nothing you photograph or write is sent anywhere.

    Why it runs on your device

    • No signal needed. Trails, parks and riverbanks often have no coverage. A cloud API would stop working exactly where you need it.
    • Your locations stay private. A journal of where you walk is sensitive. Here it only exists in this browser.
    • Free to run. No API key and no per-photo cost.
    • You can extend it. The species list is a plain file. Add the birds in your area and the model knows them right away. How
